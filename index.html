Featured project · 01

AI-Based CropRecommendation System

The live Streamlit app — screenshot, input: Karjat · hover to zoomPython · Streamlit · ML model

The problem

What to plant depends on more than instinct.

Soil nutrients, pH, temperature, humidity and rainfall all shape which crop suits a place — but that information is technical and scattered. I wanted to see whether a model could bring it into one simple screen that a farmer could read quickly.

The result is a web app: type a city or district, and it shows the soil and weather readings it is using, a recommended crop, and the model’s confidence score. A language selector sits in the sidebar.

Implemented — visible in the app

  • City / district input and a “Recommend Best Crop” action
  • Soil values: N, P, K and pH
  • Weather values: temperature, humidity, rainfall
  • A recommended crop with a model confidence score
  • Language selector in the sidebar

Proposed — not built yet

  • Testing predictions against real field data
  • Review by someone with agronomy expertise
  • Showing the top few crops, not just one
  • Explaining why a crop was chosen

What I learned: a confidence score is the model’s own output, not proof it is right. I haven’t independently validated the recommendations, so I don’t claim accuracy or real-world impact — the next real step is checking it against evidence.

Earlier work · 04

Crop yield &irrigation by AI

Smart India Hackathon

A bigger question: not just what to plant, but how to farm it well.

For the Smart India Hackathon I worked on an AI-based system concept to predict crop yield from soil data, weather patterns and historical agricultural data — built around a farmer-friendly web and mobile interface with regional-language support.

It aimed at improving agricultural productivity and resource efficiency. It sits in the same problem space as the working crop recommendation app above.

Designed

  • AI-based crop yield prediction
  • Inputs: soil data, weather patterns, historical agricultural data
  • Farmer-friendly web and mobile interface
  • Regional-language support

Proposed

  • Smart irrigation recommendations
  • Fertiliser optimisation for farmers

This is presented as a hackathon design and proposal. I’m not claiming a deployed system, a result or a measured impact.
